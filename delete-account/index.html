Delete your account

App: Studybara · Developer: MKL App Design · Effective date: October 6, 2026

Home Privacy Terms Support Delete account

In the app

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type delete to confirm.
  4. If you use Sign in with Apple, confirm with Apple when prompted.

By email

Email mklappdesign@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We verify identity by replying to that address.

What is deleted

  • Your account, sessions, and sign-in provider links
  • Group memberships, completions, and your block list
  • Group to-dos you wrote
  • Sign in with Apple authorization (when deletion succeeds in the app)

What may remain

  • Groups you owned pass to the longest-standing member, or are deleted if you were alone
  • Database backups up to 7 days
  • Server logs up to 30 days
  • Crash reports in Sentry (not linked to your account)
  • Personal study data on your device until you uninstall or clear app data

Apple users: After deletion, you can remove Studybara under Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.
