MKL App Design (“we,” “us”) publishes Studybara. This policy describes what information the app handles.

Summary

  • Signing in is optional. Personal study data stays on your device.
  • We do not sell your information or use ads or tracking.
  • Crash reports go to Sentry and are not linked to your account.

If you never sign in

Your to-dos, sessions, tags, coins, inventory, room, capy, settings, and age-check result stay on your device. Only automated crash reports may leave the device (see below).

If you sign in

We store your user id, display name, email (which may be an Apple private relay address), email-verified flag, sign-in provider and its account id, and created/updated times on our server (Microsoft Azure, United States). We never store your password, profile picture, IP address, user agent, provider tokens, or date of birth.

Sessions use a random token and expiry; expired sessions are purged within 24 hours.

Study groups

We store group names, invite codes, memberships, join dates, group to-dos (title, priority, author, times), who completed which to-do and when, and your block list.

Other members see your name, join date, and the to-dos you add or complete — never your email. If you block someone, they are hidden from you; they are not told.

Age check

Accounts require age 13+. The app asks for your date of birth on the device before sign-in; the result is stored only on the device and is not sent to us.

Crash reporting

Sentry receives device model, OS version, app version, and stack traces. Reports are not linked to your account. We disable IP storage in Sentry.

Providers

  • Microsoft Azure — hosting and database (United States)
  • Sentry — crash reports
  • Apple and Google — sign-in only

No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Retention

Account data until you delete it. Database backups up to 7 days. Server logs up to 30 days.

Deleting your account

See Delete account. Deletion removes your account, sessions, provider links, memberships, completions, block list, and group to-dos you wrote; Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked when possible. Groups you own pass to the longest-standing member or are deleted if you were alone.

If we ban an account, a hashed copy of the email may be kept to prevent re-registration.

Reports

Reports are sent from your mail app to mklappdesign@gmail.com with group and member ids; we see your address and message.

Your rights

Contact us to access, correct, or delete account data. EU/UK: we are the controller; legal bases include contract (accounts and groups) and legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, crash diagnostics). You may complain to your regulator. Data is processed in the US. Signing in is not required to use the app. We do not make automated decisions about you.

California: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Do Not Track: we do not respond to DNT signals because we do not track users.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and accounts are blocked for under-13 users on device.

Security & changes

We use HTTPS and industry-standard practices. We may update this policy; the effective date will change.
