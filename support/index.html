Email mklappdesign@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Report a group or member

In a study group, open the member menu → Report group or Block and report. Your mail app opens with the group name and id (and member id when reporting a person).

Block someone

Member menu → Block or Block and report. You will not see their name, to-dos, or completions. They are not notified.

Leave a group

Open the group → Leave group.

Delete your account

Settings → Account → Delete account, or see Delete account.
